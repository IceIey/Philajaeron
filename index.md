# Philajaeron

Philajaeron is a high fantasy world, inhabited by mostly non-magical creatures. 
Magic is a sacred art only few can master. It is strongly tied to worldly elements and heavily influenced by an [arcane dimension](/#!src/arcana/arcana.md).
Even if one has the ability to learn the use of magic, often times they don't due to the high learning barrier.

Magic is controled by [ika](#!src/arcana/arcana.md#Ika-Nodes). An invisible web that stretches through an alternate dimension and pierces through to our earthly realm, creating nodes of energy that can be harnessed and exploited. All magic is dependent on these nodes and relies on their existence. Without them, no magic would be possible.
