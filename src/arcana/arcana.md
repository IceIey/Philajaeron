# Arcane Dimension

The arcane dimension sits ontop the real world and governs the flow of [ika](#!/src/arcana/arcana.md#Ika). It is a shadowy version in black and white where magical energy flows and resides.
The flow of magic in this dimension can be experienced in bright, colorful lines hovering above the ground. Only magical inclined creatures are able to sence these lines.

Whenever the arcane dimension intersects with the real world, so called [ika nodes](#!/src/arcana/arcana.md#Ika-nodes) are created.


## Ika

Ika (jap. chikara for energy or force) is magical essence in its purest form. Several primal and secondary aspects of ika excist for various purposes.

### Ika nodes

Ika nodes are the result of the intersection of pure magical energy from the arcane dimension with worldly matter. These faintly glowing clouds are the source of all magic. Only skilled magicians are able to exploint their power fully.

## Ika aspects

Several primal and more advanced aspects of ika excist. Skilled Druids are able to extract and combine these aspects from worldy matter aided by the [ika nodes](#!/src/arcana/arcana.md#Ika-nodes) and combine their power into powerful potions.
