# Herben

Tip: Herben is a larger town on the island of [Farntry](/#!./src/farntry-isle.md) located between the [Whispering Woods](/#!./src/whispering-woods.md) and the [Veilwood Wetlands](/#!./src/veilwood-wetlands.md) close to [Echoing Knoll](/#!./src/echoing-knoll) and its caverns including [Frog Hollow Cave](/#!./src/frog-hollow-cave.md).

## Map

![HerbenMap](/images/Herben.png)

Note: Imagine cobblestone streets winding through Herben, where half-timbered houses lean against each other with their gabled roofs as if whispering secrets. The air smells of woodsmoke and roasting meat.

## Places

### *Golden Tankard Tavern*

> This bustling tavern, with its overhanging second story and brightly painted sign depicting a golden tankard overflowing with ale, sits at the heart of town. The sounds of laughter and clinking mugs spill out onto the street, inviting weary travelers inside.

### *Whispering Cauldron*

> Hidden away in a quiet alley, almost secretive in its location, is an alchemist's shop. Vials filled with bubbling potions and strange herbs line the shelves. The air inside is thick with the potent scent of a thousand different concoctions, promising both healing and peril.

### *Borin's Forge*
  
> The clang of hammer on steel echoes from the Forge. The forge itself is a sturdy stone building, its chimney belching dark smoke into the crisp air. Horseshoes hang from the eaves, and the blacksmith, a powerfully built man with soot-stained hands, works diligently at his craft.

### *Seraphinas Emporium*

> This shop for everyday needs, with its neatly stacked barrels of flour and spices and bolts of colorful fabrics, is a haven of practical goods. The shopkeeper, a kindly woman with a warm smile, greets each customer with a friendly nod.

### *Menagerie of Marvels*

> A curious zoo housing fantastical creatures. Its walls are high and sturdy, protecting the exotic animals within from escape. Visitors can catch glimpses of brightly colored birds and strange, furry beasts through the wrought iron bars. You can hear the roar of a magnificent griffon, the chattering of colorful monkeys, and the slithering of a giant constrictor snake. The air is filled with the sounds and smells of the wild, a reminder of the magic that surrounds Herben.
