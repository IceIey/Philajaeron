# Farntry Isle

Tip: Farntry Isle is the southern halfe-isle of [Calanthia](/#!./src/calanthia.md). It belongs to the kingdoms of [Ehrsburg](/#!./src/ehrsburg.md) being divided by the geographic line between the northern forest and the southern desert. The island of Farntry is connected over land to [Calanthia](/#!./src/calanthia.md) via [Rhein](/#!./src/rhein.md) and over water to [Kházdûral Havn](/#!./src/khazdural_havn.md) via [Berwihl](/#!./src/berwihl.md).
