# Farntry

Farntry is the central continent in the realm of Philajaeron. It is divided into the northern region calanthia and the southern region farnty-isle.


## Farntry Isle

Tip: Farntry Isle is the southern section of the continent [Farntry](/#!./src/calanthia.md). Its northern half belongs to the kingdom of [Ehrsburg](/#!src/kingdoms/ehrsburg.md) and its southern half to the kingdom of [Albringen](/#!src/kingdoms/albringen.md). These two kingdoms are separated by a horizontal line crossing the dangerous [Emberfang Volcano](/#!src/cities-and-regions/emberfang.md) .
The island of Farntry is connected to the northern region of the continent called [Calanthia](/#!./src/continents/farntry.md#calanthia) via [Rhein](/#!./src/cities-and-regions/rhein.md) and over water to eastern continent [Kházdûral Havn](/#!./src/continents/khazdural-havn.md) via the port city [Berwihl](/#!./src/cities-and-regions/berwihl.md).

### Map

Farntry Isle is a 400 kilometer wide and 600 kilometer long landmass and the southern part of the continent Farntry. It is divided into 3 distinct sections, each bordered by changes in natural apperances. 
![FarntryMap](/images/Farntry_Isle.png)

The north of Farntry Isle consists of a dense forest, the [Whispering Woods](/#!src/cities-and-regions/whispering-woods.md) with a few smaller villages. This section of Farntry Isle is mostly inhabited by smaller settlements of nature driven communities. The capital of the northern region is [Losbach](/#!src/cities-and-regions/losbach.md). The connection of Farntry Isle to Calanthia is only possible by travel trough [Rhein](/#!src/cities-and-regions/rhein.md), a large fortress city protecting the border between the two regions.
The central area is goverend by a large magical swamp, the [Weilvood Wetlands](/#!./src/cities-and-regions/weilwood-wetlands.md) and is bordered in the north by the swamp forest line and in the south by the [Emberfang Volcano](/#!src/cities-and-regions/emberfang.md). This region of Farntry Isle is mostly uninhabited due to its high magical activity that only few can withstand. Its regional capital is [Herben](/#!src/cities-and-regions/herben.md).
The southern area is dry and only few risk living there close to the sea. Its capital and most important port city [Berwihl](/#!src/cities-and-regions/berwihl.md) is located in the south eastern corner of Farntry Isle.
