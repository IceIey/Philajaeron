# Tavern Tales

## The Soulforged Automaton

### Session 08

- Kijel, Tamos, Zaverrax, Caverrax, Barian, Morvynn
- 2025-10-20

Here's the write up from our latest Soulforged Automaton session, in which the party collected their rewards after finishing the Booyabluk Shrine Quest. In doing so, the group discovered the brutal murder of Drimdal the alchemist (original quest giver) and forged a plan to gather information on the murder.

Begin here with [The Soulforged Automaton - Session 0](Session 0).

The Party rethought the latest occurances during a long rest at the sacred shrine. Retracing their steps carefully they manage to find their way back to Herben. They start by talking to Barin the blacksmith, eluding to him what had happened and how they managed to rescue the Booyabluk shrine. He rewards the party with gold and a discount on their weapon enhancements. As those still take some time, the group set out to talk to Drimdal the druid. Entering his alchemist shop, something feels stange, cold ... broken glass on the floor and ... Drimdal dead on the floor. Suddenly, his spiritual form appears to Kijel and Caverrax explaining how he was attacked by HIM and that HE stole the family treasure, a heartwood flask, and that HE killed him without hesitation. 

The Party ends the day at the Whispering Cauldron by talking to the newest arrival of Herben, the fisherman Raibyn Quicyne. After a short talk, they part ways and the group heads to their quarters for the night. Except Morvynn, stealthily following Raibyn into Drimdals shop. As Morvynn confronts Raibyn, he uses a second of her nattentiveness to escape into the night.

The following day, the group split with Kijel, Morvynn and Caverrax collecting their rewards from Barin the blacksmith and Tamos, Zaverrax and Barian searching the outskirts of Herben for the lost wagon. The wagon lays broken on the side of the road, so Tamos informs the others and together they repair the wagon before longresting prior to starting the investigation into Drimdals murder and the heartwood flask.
